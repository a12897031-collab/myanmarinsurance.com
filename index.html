01THE BASICS

A little clarity can
go a long way.

Insurance is an agreement intended to help protect against specified financial losses. The archived guide described two broad categories: life insurance and general insurance.

What a policy covers depends on its wording, limits, exclusions and terms. The short descriptions here are starting points for understanding the topics—not recommendations to buy a policy.

Plan for uncertainty

Coverage may help with certain costs after an unexpected event, subject to the contract.

02 /
